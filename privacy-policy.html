Privacy Policy.

How information may be handled when you visit or contact PIXRAW.

1. Introduction

PIXRAW Production respects the privacy of visitors to its website. This policy explains the general approach to information that may be shared when you visit our website or contact us.

2. Information you provide

You may choose to contact PIXRAW through WhatsApp, telephone, email or Instagram. Information you voluntarily provide through these platforms may include your name, contact details and project requirements.

This website does not provide an online enquiry form. Contact happens through the direct links published on the site.

3. How information may be used

Information you voluntarily provide may be used to:

  • Respond to your enquiries.
  • Discuss requested services and project requirements.
  • Prepare project-related communication or proposals.
  • Maintain communication related to an enquiry or service.

4. Third-party platforms

This website contains links to external platforms, including WhatsApp, Instagram and email services. When you use those links, the relevant platform may process information according to its own privacy policy and terms.

PIXRAW does not control the privacy practices or data handling policies of third-party platforms.

5. Cookies and website technologies

Website hosting providers, embedded services or website technologies may use cookies or similar technologies depending on the configuration of the deployed website. Visitors can review their browser settings to manage cookies.

6. Data security

We aim to handle information responsibly. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data retention

Information shared through communication platforms may remain available in those platforms or communication records. Retention depends on the relevant platform and the nature of the communication.

8. Your choices

You can choose which contact channel to use and what information you provide. You may contact us to ask about information you have voluntarily shared with PIXRAW.

9. Policy updates

This policy may be updated when website features, services or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.
